# Privacy Policy
_Version 4 · 11 November 2026_
_Translation provided for convenience. In case of discrepancy, the French version prevails._

This policy explains what personal data the Ostro app processes, why, for how long, with whom it is shared and what your rights are. It is addressed to the barbers and salons that use Ostro, and to their clients.

## 1. Who we are

Ostro is published by FILIPOV Dionis, EI (sole proprietor, micro-enterprise), SIRET 991 127 408 00029, 4 avenue de l'Hôpital, 06220 Vallauris, France.

Contact for any question about your data: dfmanagerfr@gmail.com.

## 2. Two different roles

- **For your Ostro account** (barber, salon, team members), we are the **data controller**.
- **For the client data** that the salon records (appointments, contact details, notes, payments, photos), **the salon is the data controller** and Ostro acts as the **processor**, solely on the salon's instructions. The rules for this processing are set out in the agreement "Processing of salon client data".

## 3. Data we process

- **Account**: name, e-mail address, Google or Apple sign-in identifier, country, language, role in the team.
- **Team member photos**: a barber's photo, added by the barber or by the account holder. Clients see it on the booking page; it is deleted as soon as it is removed or the person leaves the team.
- **Salon activity**: services and prices, opening hours, appointments, payments, expenses, statistics, team PIN codes (stored only in irreversibly encrypted form).
- **Salon clients**: name, phone number, e-mail, language, preferred reminder channel, notes, visit and payment history, any photos, consents (reminders, offers) with their date and origin.
- **Client card**: birthday (year optional), client photo, notes and up to 3 haircut photos per visit, visit-card stamps. Only the barber and the salon can see them.
- **Reviews**: the client's rating and review text after a paid visit, and the barber's reply. The booking page shows the first name, the initial of the last name and the visit date. Only reviews containing abuse or personal data can be hidden.
- **Portfolio**: haircut photos the barber shows on the booking page. A client's photo appears there only with their consent; if consent is withdrawn, their photos are removed from the portfolio at once.
- **Czechia, EET**: when sales are registered, only the DIČ, the establishment and register numbers, the sequence number, the date, time and amount are sent to the Czech tax authority. No client or service data is sent. The EET certificate stays only on the phone.
- **Messages**: reminders and confirmations sent to clients and their delivery status.
- **Technical data**: device type, app version, error logs necessary for the proper functioning of the app.

**Biometrics**: your fingerprint and face never leave your phone. Verification is performed by the device's system; Ostro receives only the answer "succeeded" or "failed".

We do not sell your data and do not use it for targeted advertising.

## 4. Why and on what legal basis

- **Providing the service** (calendar, online booking, payments, statistics, synchronization, backup): performance of the contract (terms of use).
- **Appointment reminders and confirmations** to clients: performance of the service requested by the client; the client can turn them off at any time.
- **Salon offers and news**: only with the client's **consent** (box unchecked by default), which can be withdrawn at any time, in particular by replying STOP to an SMS.
- **Security, abuse prevention, error correction**: legitimate interest.
- **Legal obligations** (accounting, responding to authorities): legal obligation.

## 5. Where data is stored

Data of salons in France and the Czech Republic is hosted in the European Union (Paris). Data of salons in the United States may be hosted in the United States. In the event of a transfer outside the European Union, we use the safeguards provided for by the GDPR (the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision).

On the phone, data is stored in the app's memory and protected by the device lock and, if you turn it on, by the Ostro lock.

## 6. How long

- **Account**: as long as the account exists; deleted within 30 days after the account is deleted, backups included.
- **Salon client data**: decided by the salon. We recommend deleting clients with no visit for 3 years.
- **Consent to offers**: kept for as long as it is valid, at most 3 years after the client's last contact.
- **Accounting records** (payments): the legal period applicable to the salon (10 years in France).
- **Technical logs**: 12 months at most.

## 7. Who we share data with

Only with providers necessary for the service, bound by contract and by confidentiality obligations:

- **Supabase, Inc.** — hosting of the database and files;
- **PowerSync (JourneyApps)** — synchronization between devices;
- **Stripe** — payment of online deposits and keeping the client's card as a guarantee against no-shows, if the salon turns it on. Stripe keeps the card number; Ostro and the salon see only the brand, the last 4 digits and the expiry date. A fee is charged only if the salon decides so after a no-show or a late cancellation, under the rules shown when booking;
- **Google and Apple** — account sign-in, if you choose this method;
- **SMS, e-mail, WhatsApp and Telegram sending providers** — for reminders and confirmations.

The up-to-date list of processors is available on request. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

## 8. Your rights

You have the rights of **access**, **rectification**, **erasure**, **restriction**, **portability** and **objection**, as well as the right to **withdraw your consent** at any time and to set directives on what happens to your data after your death.

- **Barbers and salons**: most actions are available in the app (Account → export data, delete account). For anything else, write to dfmanagerfr@gmail.com.
- **Clients of a salon**: contact the salon first, which is responsible for your data. You can also write to us; we will forward your request to the salon.

We reply within one month.

**Complaint**: you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country — in France the **CNIL** (3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, cnil.fr), in the Czech Republic the **ÚOOÚ** (Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, uoou.gov.cz).

## 9. U.S. residents

Depending on your state (for example California), you may ask to know, correct or delete your personal information. Ostro does not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

**SMS**: reminder SMS messages are sent only to clients who gave their number for an appointment; promotional SMS messages only with express consent. Reply **STOP** to stop receiving messages, **HELP** for help. Message and data rates may apply. SMS numbers and consents are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 10. Children

Ostro is intended for professionals and is not intended for persons under 16. A child's appointment is made by a parent or legal guardian.

## 11. Security

Encrypted connections, access limited by role in the team, irreversibly encrypted PIN codes, strict separation of data between salons, app lock by biometrics or device passcode. In the event of a data breach presenting a risk, we notify the persons concerned and the competent authority within the legal deadlines.

## 12. Changes

If this policy changes significantly, the app will tell you and ask you to accept it again. The version and date appear at the top of the document.
