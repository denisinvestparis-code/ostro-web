# Processing of Salon Client Data
_Version 1 · 7 November 2026_
_Translation provided for convenience. In case of discrepancy, the French version prevails._

This agreement, compliant with Article 28 of the GDPR, forms part of the terms of use. It applies between the salon or barber who holds the account (**the Controller**) and FILIPOV Dionis, EI, publisher of Ostro (**the Processor**).

## 1. Purpose and duration

The Processor processes the personal data of the Controller's clients solely to provide Ostro: calendar, online booking, reminders and confirmations, deposits, payments, statistics, synchronization and backup. The agreement lasts as long as the account.

## 2. Data and data subjects

- **Data subjects**: clients and prospects of the salon, team members.
- **Data**: identity, phone number, e-mail, language, appointments, visit and payment history, notes, photos, consents.
- The Controller undertakes not to record sensitive data within the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR.

## 3. Obligations of the Processor

The Processor:

- processes the data **only on documented instructions** from the Controller (settings and actions made in the app constitute instructions);
- ensures that persons authorized to process the data are bound by **confidentiality**;
- implements appropriate **security measures**: encryption of connections, separation of data between salons, role-based access control, encrypted PIN codes, backups;
- **assists the Controller** in responding to requests to exercise data subjects' rights (export, correction, deletion of a client from the app);
- **notifies** the Controller of any personal data breach **without undue delay and at the latest 72 hours** after becoming aware of it;
- assists the Controller with impact assessments and consultation of the authority, if necessary;
- makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this agreement and allows reasonable audits, on written request and with 30 days' notice.

## 4. Sub-processors

The Controller authorizes the use of the sub-processors listed in the privacy policy (hosting, synchronization, payment, message sending). The Processor informs the Controller of any addition or replacement; the Controller may object on legitimate grounds and, failing agreement, delete their account. Each sub-processor is bound by equivalent obligations.

## 5. Transfers outside the European Union

Data of salons in the European Union is hosted in the European Union. Any transfer outside the European Union is governed by the safeguards of Article 46 of the GDPR (standard contractual clauses) or by an adequacy decision.

## 6. End of processing

When the account is deleted, the Controller may first export their data. The Processor then deletes the data, backups included, within 30 days, unless a legal retention obligation applies.

## 7. Obligations of the Controller

The Controller guarantees that they have a legal basis for each processing operation, inform their clients, obtain their consent for promotional offers and respond to their requests.
