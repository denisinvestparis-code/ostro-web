# Your Data When Booking
_Version 2 · 11 November 2026_
_Translation provided for convenience. In case of discrepancy, the French version prevails._

You are booking with a salon that uses Ostro. **The salon is responsible for your data**; Ostro is the booking tool it uses.

## What we record

Your name, your phone number, your e-mail if you provide it, the language, the chosen appointment, the preferred reminder method and, where applicable, the deposit paid. If the salon asks for a card to hold your booking, Stripe keeps the card: the salon sees only its brand and last 4 digits and can charge a fee only for a no-show or a late cancellation, under the rules shown before you book.

## Why

- **Managing your appointment** and sending you the confirmation and reminders. These messages are part of the service; you can turn off reminders when booking or by asking the salon.
- **Salon offers and news**: only if you tick the box provided. It is not required to book. You can unsubscribe at any time, for example by replying **STOP** to an SMS.

Your data is neither sold nor used for targeted advertising.

## How long

The salon keeps your record as long as you are a client; we recommend that it delete records with no visit for 3 years. Payments are kept for the legal accounting period.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw your consent to offers. Contact the salon, or write to dfmanagerfr@gmail.com: we will forward your request to the salon.

You can also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (in France: the CNIL, cnil.fr).

To learn more, see Ostro's privacy policy.
